Energy Sustainablity Hub for Metropolitan Water District


In 2020, the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California (MWD) published a new Energy Sustainability Plan (ESP) to formulate actions and strategies that best adapt to future wholesale and retail energy market changes. Funded by the Geodesign Fellowship, this collaboration developed a central geospatial database (“Energy Sustainability Hub”) to support MWD’s ESP implementation. The Hub also integrates a web app that presents and tracks ESP progress, along with interactive “Water-Energy-Climate nexus” data dashboards for pumping, electricity consumption, and greenhouse gas emissions of MWD facility plants operation.

Acknowledgements
