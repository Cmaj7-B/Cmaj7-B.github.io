Tracking network dynamics of USC's sustainability initiatives


The project captures the diverse network across the campus to reflect the past and current efforts in sustainability, as well as the formation of new and expanded networks that are direct outcomes from the Presidential Working Group initiatives. A dataset of sustainability initiatives was constructed by collecting open-source information and internally shared PWG meeting notes/reports.
