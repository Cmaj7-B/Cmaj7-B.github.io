Methodology

Inspired by Receiptify, the interactive carbon calculator prints a rough estimation of how much carbon emission per year we may reduce via daily activities. Calculations are based on the references below.


List of References

Cho, Renee. The 35 Easiest Ways to Reduce Your Carbon Footprint. Dec. 27, 2018. Columbia Climate School. [link]

Jones M., Christopher an Kammen M. Daniel. Quantifying Carbon Footprint Reduction Opportunities for U.S. Households and Communities. Environ. Sci. Technol. 2011, 45, 9, 4088–4095.

Vandenbergh, Michael P., et al. "Individual Carbon Emissions: The Low-Hanging Fruit." UCLA Law Review, vol. 55, no. 6, August 2008, p. 1701-1758.

Wynes, S. and Nicholas, K., 2017. The climate mitigation gap: education and government recommendations miss the most effective individual actions. Environmental Research Letters, 12(7), p.074024. [link]
